# Ronan Schoolhouse

## Our Vision

### A beloved school. A shared future.
A place for community. Again.
Our vision is to bring this beloved former school back to life with affordable senior apartments, community event space, and a family funhouse.

### Built for a school. Reimagined for the community.
A family, an old school, and a future worth figuring out.
Behind Ronan Schoolhouse is our family, kids included. We’re taking this on with a family budget, plenty of questions, and a willingness to learn. Some days, progress means figuring out the next step.

We don’t have every step mapped out, but we know what we want this place to become: affordable homes for older adults, space for neighbors to gather, and a funhouse for families. This school already means so much to the community. We want to give it a chance to serve that community again.

## What We’re Working Toward

One schoolhouse. So many possibilities. A place to live, a place to gather, a place to play.

### 01 / LIVE: Affordable senior apartments
Our plan starts with homes for older adults: an affordable place to live in a building with deep community roots.

### 02 / GATHER: Community event space
We envision space for neighbors to come together, celebrate milestones, and make new memories.

### 03 / PLAY: Family funhouse
A place for play, laughter, and time together that brings the energy of family life back into the old school.

## Building Our Next Chapter

### Big possibilities. Room for you.
We’re a family with children taking on a very big building. Our budget is a family budget, and we’re figuring out plenty as we go. The vision is clear: give this school a future serving the community. These are the next practical pieces we’re working on. 

We’ll share what we’re learning and confirm plans as they take shape.

## Upcoming Projects

### 01 / PLUMBING: Plumbing insulation and freeze protection
Status: Upcoming project
We are focusing on plumbing insulation and freeze protection for water lines to help protect the water systems during cold weather.

Potential ways to participate: Supply plumbing insulation or water line protection products and explore opportunities to feature them in our project videos and social posts.

### 02 / ELECTRICAL: Tracing electrical branch circuits
Status: Upcoming project
Our focus is tracing the electrical branch circuits so we can better understand how power is distributed throughout the school.

Potential ways to participate: Supply circuit tracing tools or related equipment and explore opportunities to showcase your products as we document this project.

### 03 / INDOOR ENVIRONMENT: Air quality and humidity control
Status: Upcoming project
We are focusing on air quality and humidity control as part of caring for the building and preparing for its next chapter.

Potential ways to participate: Supply humidity monitoring, dehumidification, or air quality equipment and explore opportunities to feature your products in our project updates.
